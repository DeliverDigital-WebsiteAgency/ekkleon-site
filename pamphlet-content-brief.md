## PANEL — THE PROBLEM

**Every small business website is a compromise.**

Page builders like Wix and Squarespace are easy to edit but hit a ceiling fast — generic templates, weak SEO control, and slower load times as the site grows. Traditional WordPress can look great, but the same system serving your content is also exposed to the public internet, which is where most WordPress security and speed problems start. And almost no small business site is built with how people actually search today — through ChatGPT and Perplexity, not just Google.

You shouldn't have to choose between *easy to update* and *actually good.*

## PANEL — THE SOLUTION

**One system. Both halves done right.**

This starter kit splits your website into two parts that each do what they're best at:

- **The back end is WordPress** — the same dashboard you already know, for writing pages, swapping photos, and adding services.
- **The front end is custom-built and modern** — fast, secure, and structured for both search engines and AI assistants.

Edit a headline or paragraph in WordPress, and it's live on the actual site in about 30 seconds. No developer, no rebuild, no waiting on a ticket.

## PANEL — WHY NOT [ALTERNATIVE]?

**"Why not Wix or Squarespace?"**
Fast to start, but capped fast — generic templates, limited SEO control, and slower as your site grows. This kit keeps the "I can edit it myself" simplicity without the ceiling.

**"Why not a normal WordPress site?"**
Traditional WordPress exposes the same system that holds your content directly to the public internet — that's where most of its speed and security problems come from. Here, you still get the WordPress dashboard, just without WordPress's usual baggage.

**"Why not a fully custom site?"**
A fully custom build starts from zero every time. This kit starts from a proven, working foundation — so you get custom results without paying to reinvent the plumbing.
